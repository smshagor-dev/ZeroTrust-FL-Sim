## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers are expected to:

- keep required CI and release gates healthy
- review code for correctness, security-boundary changes, compatibility, and reproducibility
- avoid merging security-sensitive changes by bypassing required checks
- keep threat-model and architecture documentation aligned with implementation
- triage vulnerability reports through the private process described in `SECURITY.md`
- distinguish measured benchmark results from estimates or theoretical claims
- preserve release provenance and immutable version history

## Adding maintainers

A contributor may be added as a maintainer after sustained high-quality contributions, demonstrated understanding of the project's security and federated-learning assumptions, and approval from the Project Lead. Access should follow least privilege and be removed when no longer needed.

## Conflict of interest

Maintainers should disclose material conflicts that could bias a security assessment, benchmark claim, dependency decision, or review. When practical, another maintainer or independent reviewer should handle the affected decision.
